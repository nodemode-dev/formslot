# Code of conduct

FormSlot is built in the open by people who want coding agents to reuse what already exists instead of writing it again. This page says how we treat each other while doing it. It applies to the repository, its issues, pull requests and discussions, and to anyone speaking for the project elsewhere.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, with newcomers above all. Everyone started somewhere.
- Argue about code, designs and ideas, never about people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding otherwise.
- Accept correction gracefully, and give it kindly and specifically.
- Credit the work of others.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory language or conduct, on any grounds.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission.
- Deliberately derailing discussions, or sustained disruption of any kind.

## Reporting

Report anything that breaks this code by opening an [issue](https://github.com/nodemode-dev/formslot/issues), or by reaching the maintainers of the [nodemode-dev](https://github.com/nodemode-dev) organisation on GitHub. The maintainers are listed in the [README](README.md#maintainers) and in [`.github/CODEOWNERS`](.github/CODEOWNERS).

## What happens next

The maintainers review every report and decide what is fair: a private word, a public note, removal of content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the project's spaces. People who report in good faith will not face retaliation.
